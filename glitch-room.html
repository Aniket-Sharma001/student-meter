HOW TO PLAY

A room. A rule. One thing out of place.

  1. Choose a difficulty and begin. The room's first button never gives the same response twice in a row.
  2. Read the room rule, then tap the object that breaks it before the timer runs out.
  3. Wrong guesses cost a little time, not the whole game. Find three glitches to escape.
  4. Score rewards quick, accurate eyes. Your best score, games played and fastest time are saved on this device only.

No camera, microphone, location or personal device data is accessed. Game statistics use local storage only.
