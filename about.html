A student-made companion

Life has a lot of tabs open.
Let's make it easier.

STUDENT METER is a fun and useful digital companion for diploma and college students. It brings study tracking, focus sessions, routines, money notes, mood check-ins and goals together in one friendly place.

📚 Learn at your pace🌱 Progress over perfection🔒 Local browser storage
What matters here

Useful, encouraging, yours.

This project is designed to make everyday planning feel lighter: set small goals, see your habits take shape and celebrate the effort you put in. Your entries are stored in your browser using localStorage; there is no account or backend.

This website is an independent student project and is not affiliated with any college or organization.
